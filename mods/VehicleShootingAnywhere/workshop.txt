Companion to Hunterz' Vehicle Shooting: lets passengers shoot from ANY seat of ANY vehicle (DayZ Expansion helicopters included) with ANY firearm, instead of pistols in vanilla cars only. A 40-line script override, nothing of Vehicle Shooting is repacked.

Requires: Vehicle Shooting (Hunterz & Dave) and Survivor Animations (TheDaveCZE & Hunterz). Load after Vehicle Shooting. Server and client.

Credits: Hunterz and Dave for Vehicle Shooting, which does all the real work; TheDaveCZE for Survivor Animations; Helicopter Shooting Red Falcon for showing the same trick for RedFalcon helis.
